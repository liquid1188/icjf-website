March 1, 2018 – Five Dubia: Answers from Amoris Laetitia Itself (Dr. Fastiggi)

March 1, 2018 · Catholic Social Teaching

http://www.lastampa.it/2018/03/09/vaticaninsider/eng/documents/responding-to-the-five-dubia-from-amoris-laetitia-itself-0wrtch9RSgo5NGM9pyTm1L/pagina.html

“I know much attention has been given to the five dubia of the four Cardinals, and some people are upset and angry that Pope Francis has not responded to them. I can’t speak for the Holy Father, but it might be that he has not responded because there is no need to do so. If we make  the effort, we can find the responses in Amoris Laetitia itself—in what it says and what it does not say.”
