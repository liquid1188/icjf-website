April 14, 2020 – Bishops dedicate Americas to Our Lady of Guadalupe during pandemic

April 14, 2020 · Bishops

Latin American and Caribbean bishops dedicate the Americas to Our Lady of Guadalupe
The dedication of the region to Our Lady of Guadalupe — often called “Empress of the Americas” — revived a tradition of seeking her intervention in times of crisis and during pandemics. Churches throughout the region joined in the livestreamed Mass by ringing their bells at noon Mexico City time.
  • U.S. bishops deliver Easter messages of hope to online congregations.
  • Bishop Daniel R Jenky says praying over diocese from helicopter may give some comfort.
