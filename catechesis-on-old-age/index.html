Pope Francis: Catechesis on Old Age (2022)

Pope Francis’s series of catecheses on old age, given at the General Audiences of 2022, collected in one booklet published by Opus Dei.

Read the Catechesis on Old Age (PDF)

Contents

  1. 1. The grace of time and the bond between age and life
  2. 2. Longevity: symbol and opportunity
  3. 3. Old age, a resource for lighthearted youth
  4. 4. Farewell and inheritance: memory and testimony
  5. 5. Fidelity to God’s visitation for the next generation
  6. 6. "Honour your father and your mother:" love for the gift of life
  7. 7. Naomi: the alliance between the generations that opens up the future
  8. 8. Eleazar: consistency of the faith, honourable inheritance
  9. 9. Judith: admirable in youth, generous in old age
  10. 10. Job: the trial of faith, the blessing of waiting
  11. 11. Ecclesiastes: the uncertain night of meaning and of things in life
  12. 12. "Forsake me not when my strength is spent" (Ps 71:9)
  13. 13. Nicodemus: "How can a man be born when he is old?" (Jn 3:4)
  14. 14. The joyful service of faith that is learned in gratitude
  15. 15. Peter and John
  16. 16. "I go to prepare a place for you:" a time projected towards fulfilment
  17. 17. The "Ancient of days:" reassurance and life that never dies again

Source: opusdei.org
