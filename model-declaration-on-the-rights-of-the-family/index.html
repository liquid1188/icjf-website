A Model Declaration on the Rights of the Family

A Translation of the Charter on the Rights of the Family into International Human Rights Language

NOTE: This initiative was developed to assist those working on the drafting of documents, in the political field, where the Charter on the Rights of the Family has been erroneously dismissed, by some, as a religious document. Based on the Charter on the Rights of the Family, this Model Declaration is an example of how the Charter is of ongoing relevance in the field of international human rights.

Jane Adolphe, B.A., LL.B/B.C.L., J.C.L/J.C.D., then Associate Professor of Law, Ave Maria School of Law, Naples, Florida presented this document at the XXI PLENARY ASSEMBLY OF THE PONTIFICAL COUNCIL FOR THE FAMILY DEVOTED TO THE XXX ANNIVERSARY OF THE CHARTER OF THE RIGHTS OF THE FAMILY (Rome, 23 to 25 October 2013).

The Model Declaration was published by the Forum under its former name, the International Center on Law, Life, Faith and Family (ICOLF).
