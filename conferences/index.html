October 1-3, 2026, A Natural Law Discussion: From the Ancients to Contemporary Thought. The Crisis and Renewal of Natural Law in the Modern World, international conference co-hosted by ICJF with the Università Europea di Roma, Lumen De Lumine Foundation, Centro Studi Livatino, Iuris Naturalis Societas, European Centre for Law and Justice, and Fundación Veritas, held at the Università Europea di Roma, Rome, Italy. Opening lecture by Cardinal Raymond Leo Burke.

ICJF participants included President Jane Adolphe, Board Member John Klink, and Advisors Iain Benson, Fulvio Di Blasi, Daniel B. Gallagher, Rev. Piotr Mazurkiewicz, Diego Poole, Grégor Puppinck, and Aleksander Stępkowski.
